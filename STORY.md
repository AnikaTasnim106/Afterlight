# AFTERLIGHT — story outline (8 levels)

Rules of the story: origin stays mysterious (no secret-lab conspiracy), military is
trying to help, moderate horror, hopeful ending. All names English.

## Level 1 — The First Evacuation  ✅ built
Alex & June's apartment → neighbours → June evacuated → the six rules → June's voice
on the radio → stairwell fight with Nina (baby) and Mr. Daniels → rooftop helicopter,
meets Mara. Cliffhanger: "breach on the east fence" at Relief Zone Bravo.

## Level 2 — Relief Zone Bravo  ✅ built
The breach was "contained". The stadium camp looks safe: tents, food lines, soldiers.
- Registration: June was here — transferred this morning on **Convoy 7 to Harbor Point
  Medical Campus** ("specialist screening"). Mara's brother **Theo** isn't in the records.
- The camp: Dr. Elena Ward (camp doctor, part of the Harbor Point research team) treats
  Mr. Daniels and mentions "some patients respond to a compound — early days".
  Sam (a kid) wears Theo's guitar pick: Theo volunteered with the medics and left on
  Convoy 7 too. Alex and Mara now have the same destination.
- Night: the east fence really fails. Escort Dr. Ward and her sample case to the motor
  pool with Mara covering. Sgt. Hale gives them a truck.
- Cliffhanger: June on 146.52 — "Harbor Point isn't letting anyone leave."

## Level 3 — Dead City  ✅ built
The truck dies downtown. On foot through streets, a pharmacy, a subway entrance.
Stealth (rules matter: noise, don't approach), scavenging, a group of desperate
survivors (not villains) — share supplies or not. Mara's backstory: she was supposed
to pick Theo up the night it started.

## Level 4 — The River  ✅ built
Bridges are blocked. Find a boat at the marina, cross at dusk. June's next transmission
gives a ward number. Mara and Alex start trusting each other.

## Level 5 — Harbor Point  ✅ built
A medical campus under quarantine. Get inside (records office, service tunnels).
Dr. Ward's team: the compound works on early infections. June is in the quarantine wing.
Theo is alive — working as an orderly.

## Level 6 — The Quarantine Wing  ✅ built
An outbreak inside the wing. Rescue June (her radio skills open the locked wards).
Mara & Theo reunite. Mr. Daniels-style injured patients need the compound.

## Level 7 — The Signal  ✅ built
June boosts the hospital antenna; Alex defends the roof while the treatment protocol is
broadcast to every safe zone. Distribution begins.

## Level 8 — Afterlight (epilogue)  ✅ built
Weeks later: safe zones reopen, recovery teams, Nina reunited with her husband,
Mr. Daniels walking without a limp, Mara & Theo, Alex & June back home — the six rules
pinned on the fridge.
